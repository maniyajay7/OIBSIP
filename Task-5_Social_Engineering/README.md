# Task 5: Social Engineering Attacks — Research Report

## 📌 Objective
Research and document the major types of social engineering attacks, the psychology behind them, real-world case studies (classic and 2025-2026), and defense strategies.

## 📄 Report Contents

### Attack Types Covered (8 Types)

| # | Attack Type | Description |
|---|------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Phishing** | Email-based attacks — mass, spear, whaling, and clone phishing |
| 2 | **Vishing** | Voice phishing via phone calls — including TOAD and AI voice clones |
| 3 | **Smishing** | SMS-based phishing — fake delivery, bank, and toll messages |
| 4 | **Pretexting** | Fabricated scenarios to extract information over multiple interactions |
| 5 | **Baiting** | Exploiting curiosity — infected USBs, fake downloads, ClickFix |
| 6 | **Tailgating** | Physical SE — following authorized personnel through secured doors |
| 7 | **Quid Pro Quo** | Offering fake "help" in exchange for credentials or access |
| 8 | **Deepfake SE** | AI-generated video/voice impersonation (NEW 2025-2026 category) |
